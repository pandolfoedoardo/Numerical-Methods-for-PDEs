Numerical analysis of the stationary convection-diffusion PDE in advection-dominated regimes ($\beta = (1, 3)^T$) relative to the diffusion coefficient $D \in [10^{-3}, 1]$. The study analyzes the violent spurious oscillations and stability loss of the unstabilized standard Galerkin method when exceeding the critical grid pseudo-Péclet number threshold ($\text{Pec} \ge 4.4$). To restore solution regularity while preserving variational consistency, the Streamline Upwind Petrov-Galerkin (SUPG) stabilization scheme is implemented by introducing artificial diffusion strictly along streamlines and calibrating the parameter $\delta$ to avoid both residual oscillations (under-stabilization) and non-physical oversmoothing (over-diffusion).
