Numerical solution of the two-dimensional elliptic Poisson equation on a bounded Lipschitz domain with mixed boundary conditions (homogeneous Dirichlet and Neumann fluxes). The implementation relies on the discrete weak formulation using continuous piecewise linear $\mathcal{P}^1$ finite elements over uniformly refined triangulations, assembling the local stiffness matrix in exact analytical form and enforcing Dirichlet constraints via the penalty method. The project experimentally validates the theoretical a priori asymptotic convergence rates by evaluating errors in the $L^2$-norm and $H^1$-seminorm against a manufactured analytical solution, confirming the expected convergence orders of $\mathcal{O}(h^2)$ for $L^2$ (error reduction factor of 4) and $\mathcal{O}(h)$ for $H^1$ (error reduction factor of 2).
